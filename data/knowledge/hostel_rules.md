# Hostel and Campus Rules

## Hostel Accommodation

Hostel accommodation is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Priority is given to students from outside the city. Hostel fees must be paid at the beginning of each semester along with tuition fees. Students who fail to pay hostel fees within 15 days of the deadline will lose their allotted room.

## Hostel Timings

Students must return to the hostel by 9:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 PM on weekends. Late entry requires written permission from the hostel warden. Students leaving the hostel for more than 24 hours must fill out an outing form with their destination, contact number, and expected return date. The form must be signed by the hostel warden.

## Hostel Facilities

Each hostel room is furnished with a bed, study table, chair, and wardrobe. Common facilities include a mess hall, reading room, recreation room, and laundry area. Wi-Fi is available in all hostel buildings from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Hostel mess provides breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and dinner. The mess menu is decided by the mess committee and rotated weekly. Students with dietary restrictions or allergies should inform the mess committee at the beginning of the semester.

## Campus Facilities

The campus includes a central library, computer labs, sports facilities, a medical center, a cafeteria, and a student activity center. The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends. During examination periods, the library hours are extended to 11:00 PM.

Computer labs are accessible to all students during working hours (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM). Extended lab access during project deadlines must be approved by the lab coordinator.

## Anti-Ragging Policy

The college has a zero-tolerance policy towards ragging in any form. Any student found involved in ragging will face immediate disciplinary action, which may include suspension or expulsion from the college and hostel. Students who experience or witness ragging should report it immediately to the anti-ragging committee, hostel warden, or use the anonymous complaint hotline.

## Campus Parking

Parking is available for students with valid parking passes. Parking passes can be obtained from the administrative office. Two-wheeler parking is free. Four-wheeler parking requires a monthly fee. Vehicles without valid passes may be towed.

## Maintenance and Complaints

Students may report maintenance issues such as plumbing, electrical faults, broken furniture, or non-functioning air conditioning through the campus maintenance portal or by contacting the hostel warden. Maintenance requests are addressed within 48 hours for non-urgent issues and within 4 hours for emergency issues such as water supply disruption or electrical faults.

## Code of Conduct

Students are expected to maintain discipline and decorum on campus. Damage to college property will result in a fine and disciplinary action. Use of alcohol, drugs, or tobacco products on campus is strictly prohibited.
