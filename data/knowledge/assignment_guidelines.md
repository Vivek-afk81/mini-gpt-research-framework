# Assignment Guidelines

## Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted by the deadline specified by the course instructor. Late submissions will incur a penalty of 10% per day for up to 3 days after the deadline. Assignments submitted more than 3 days late will not be accepted and will receive zero marks.

## Assignment Format

Unless otherwise specified by the instructor, assignments should follow these formatting guidelines:
- Typed assignments should use A4 size paper, 12-point Times New Roman or Arial font, 1.5 line spacing, and 1-inch margins on all sides.
- Handwritten assignments should be written in blue or black ink on ruled or A4 sheets, with the student's name, roll number, subject, and date on the first page.
- All assignments must include a cover page with the student's name, roll number, section, subject name, subject code, assignment title, and submission date.

## Plagiarism Policy

The college has a strict anti-plagiarism policy. All typed assignments will be checked using plagiarism detection software. The maximum allowed similarity index is 20%. Assignments with a similarity index above 20% will be returned for revision. Assignments with a similarity index above 40% will receive zero marks and may result in disciplinary action.

## Group Assignments

Group assignments must have a maximum of 4 members unless the instructor specifies otherwise. Each group must clearly indicate the contribution of each member. All members of the group will receive the same marks unless the instructor determines that contributions were significantly unequal.

## Assignment Weightage

Assignments typically carry 10% to 20% of the total marks for a subject, as part of the continuous internal assessment component. The exact weightage is determined by the course instructor and communicated at the beginning of the semester.

## Submission Modes

Assignments may be submitted in one of the following ways as specified by the instructor:
- Physical submission to the department office or faculty during class.
- Online submission through the college learning management system or email.
- For online submissions, only PDF format is accepted unless otherwise stated.

## Assignment Feedback

Faculty members will provide feedback and return graded assignments within 2 weeks of the submission deadline. Students who wish to discuss their grades may schedule a meeting with the instructor during office hours.
