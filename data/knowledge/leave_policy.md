# Leave Policy

## Types of Leave

Students may apply for the following types of leave:

### Casual Leave
Students are entitled to a maximum of 10 days of casual leave per semester. Casual leave may be taken for personal reasons, family events, or minor illnesses. Casual leave must be applied for at least 1 day in advance, except in case of emergencies.

### Medical Leave
Medical leave is granted for illness or hospitalization. Students must submit a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner within 5 working days of resuming classes. Medical leave exceeding 5 consecutive days requires approval from the Head of Department. There is no upper limit on medical leave, but extended absences may affect attendance eligibility.

### Duty Leave
Students participating in college-approved events, workshops, inter-college competitions, NCC, NSS activities, or sports at the university or national level are eligible for duty leave. Duty leave does not count against the student's attendance percentage. A duty leave certificate signed by the event coordinator or faculty advisor must be submitted within 3 days of the event.

### Emergency Leave
In case of family emergencies such as serious illness or death of an immediate family member, students may apply for emergency leave of up to 7 days. Supporting documents must be submitted upon return.

## Leave Application Process

All leave applications must be submitted through the student portal or in writing to the class coordinator. The application should include the type of leave, the dates, and the reason. Supporting documents, where applicable, must be attached.

Leave applications for more than 3 days require approval from the Head of Department. Applications for less than 3 days require approval from the class coordinator or mentor.

## Impact on Attendance

All leave except duty leave counts as an absence for attendance calculation purposes. However, approved medical leave with valid documentation may be considered for attendance relaxation as per the attendance policy.

## Unauthorized Absence

Students who remain absent without applying for leave will be marked absent. Repeated unauthorized absences may result in a warning letter being sent to the student and their parent or guardian.
