## Supplementary Examinations

Supplementary examinations for the Odd Semester will be conducted in February 2025. Supplementary examinations for the Even Semester will be conducted in July 2025. The exact schedule will be published 2 weeks before the examinations.

## Important Deadlines

- Fee payment deadline: Within 15 days of semester registration.
- Scholarship application deadline: Within 30 days of semester start.
- Subject drop deadline: Within 4 weeks of classes beginning.
- Course feedback submission: Last week of teaching before examinations.
- Hostel accommodation application: 2 weeks before semester registration.
- ID card issuance: Within 2 weeks of registration for new students.
