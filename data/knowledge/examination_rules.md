# Examination Rules

## Examination Schedule

The college conducts two types of examinations each semester: Mid-Semester Examinations (MSE) and End-Semester Examinations (ESE). The schedule for both examinations is published on the college website and notice board at least 2 weeks before the commencement of exams.

## Examination Pattern

Mid-Semester Examinations carry 30% of the total marks for theory subjects. End-Semester Examinations carry 50% of the total marks. The remaining 20% is allocated to continuous internal assessment, which includes assignments, quizzes, and class participation.

For practical subjects, the internal practical assessment carries 60% and the end-semester practical examination carries 40%.

## Hall Ticket

Students must download their examination hall ticket from the student portal at least 3 days before the examination begins. No student will be allowed to appear for the examination without a valid hall ticket. Students who have not paid their examination fees will not be issued a hall ticket.

## Materials Allowed in Examinations

Students are allowed to carry only blue or black ink pens, pencils, erasers, rulers, and approved calculators (non-programmable, non-graphing) unless otherwise specified by the subject faculty. Mobile phones and smartwatches are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall.

## Supplementary and Re-examinations

Students who fail a subject in the End-Semester Examination may appear for a supplementary examination conducted in the following semester. A maximum of 3 attempts are allowed per subject including the original attempt.

Students who wish to improve their grade in a subject may apply for a grade improvement examination. Grade improvement is allowed only for subjects where the student has scored a C grade or below.

## Malpractice

Any student found engaging in unfair means during examinations will face disciplinary action ranging from cancellation of the paper to suspension from the college for one academic year, depending on the severity of the offense.

## Grace Marks

A provision of up to 5 grace marks per subject is available for students who narrowly fail. Grace marks are awarded at the discretion of the examination committee and are applicable only in End-Semester Examinations.
